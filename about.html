About Achievers University

The first private university in Ondo and Ekiti States

Our Story

Achievers University, Owo, was established in 2007 and commenced academic activities on 2nd April 2008 with 126 students. It is located on a 187-hectare permanent site at KM 1, Idasen-Ute Road, Owo, Ondo State, Nigeria.

The University sprang from the Achievers Group of Education and Training Organization. It is owned by Hon. (Dr.) Bode Ayorinde and other notable shareholders.

Since inception the University has graduated 14 sets of students who have participated in the National Youth Service Corps (NYSC) Scheme. The 2025/2026 session ushers in the 19th set of undergraduates.
