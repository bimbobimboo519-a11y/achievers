General Requirements

UTME Candidates

  • Minimum JAMB score of 150 (private universities cut-off). Higher for Medicine & Surgery.
  • Five O’Level credits including English Language and Mathematics (and relevant subjects) in not more than two sittings.
  • Candidates must have chosen Achievers University or be ready to change institution.

Direct Entry

  • A’Level, NCE, OND, HND or equivalent in relevant fields.
  • Professional certificates (ACCA, ACIB, etc.) may be accepted for Accounting & Business Administration.

How to Apply

  1. Visit apply.achievers.edu.ng
  2. Create an account with a valid email and phone number.
  3. Complete the application form and make the required payment.
  4. Attend screening (daily at the University Registry, Owo).
Beware of fraudsters! Only make payments through official portals. Verify all information on www.achievers.edu.ng.
